  • 2024-09-05
    泽风计划立项获批Project Zephyr approved

    经馆务会议审议并报主管部门备案，「泽风计划」正式立项。项目周期两年，分修复、著录、数字化加工与开放利用四个阶段实施。Following review by the archives committee and filing with the supervising authority, Project Zephyr was formally established. The project runs two years, in four phases: repair, description, digitization, and public access.

  • 2024-11-28
    档案交接完成Archive handover complete

    邮电系统移交档案 137 卷清点入库，其中磁带 41 盘、纸质卷宗 96 卷。清点中发现部分卷宗存在复印件混入情况，原件待补。137 volumes transferred from the postal system were checked and placed in storage: 41 tapes and 96 paper files. During checking, photocopies were found mixed into some files; the originals are still to be recovered.

  • 2025-06-30
    首批数字化档案开放阅览First digitized archives open for reading

    首批 1946 年档案（设备登记表及附件共 3 卷）开放在线阅览。读者可于《馆藏目录》中检索档号后进入阅览。The first batch of 1946 archives (equipment registry and annexes, 3 volumes) is open for online reading. Readers may search the file numbers in the catalog and proceed to reading.

  • 2025-10-21
    关于暂停部分 1986 年卷带上线阅览的公告Notice: some 1986 reels held back from release

    修复组在比对中发现，第二卷录音（REEL-B）的部分采样与第一卷（REEL-A）完全一致，疑似复制卷。暂停该批卷带上线。已在阅览中的相关卷宗维持原状。During comparison the repair team found that part of the second recording (REEL-B) is identical, sample for sample, to the first (REEL-A), suggesting a copy. The affected reels are held back from release. Volumes already in reading remain as they were.

  • 2026-01-09
    关于 CORE-17 目录的说明Note on the CORE-17 directory

    数字化处理过程中发现，部分不同年代的档案索引指向同一构建目录（编号 CORE-17）。经评估，该目录系历史处理系统遗留，不属对外开放范围。相关删除申请共三次，均因不符合档案管理规范予以驳回。另有 1998 年勘测件一条处理链（CORE-18）经复核不在馆藏范围，已盖注销章。During digitization it was found that indexes from different eras point to the same build directory (numbered CORE-17). On assessment, the directory is a legacy of the former processing system and outside the public scope. Three deletion requests were filed; all were rejected as contrary to records-management rules. One 1998 survey item's processing chain (CORE-18) was reviewed, found outside the holdings, and stamped revoked.

  • 2026-06-14
    关于档案 A 附件第二枚印章的说明Note on the second stamp on the Archive A annex

    有读者来信询问档案 A 附件上的第二枚 1946 年印章（KING EDWARD VII COLLEGE OF MEDICINE）。经查，该印章真实存在、墨迹与年代相符，属另一机构系统的公文用印，与本批无线电档案无涉。读者不必在此印章上继续花费时间。A reader wrote in about the second 1946 stamp on the Archive A annex (KING EDWARD VII COLLEGE OF MEDICINE). On inspection the stamp is genuine, the ink consistent with the period; it belongs to another institution's official correspondence and has nothing to do with these radio archives. Readers need spend no further time on this stamp.

  • 2026-03-17
    关于修复组林澄同志岗位调整的情况说明Statement on the reassignment of Lin Cheng, repair group

    林澄同志自 2026 年 3 月起未再到岗，多次尝试联系未果，已按人事管理相关规定处理。原定于 3 月开放的第三批卷宗暂缓上线。Lin Cheng has not reported to work since March 2026; repeated attempts to reach her failed, and the matter has been handled under personnel rules. The third batch of volumes, due to open in March, is postponed.

  • 2026-05-06
    关于网传「许静仪」相关信息的说明Note on online rumors concerning "Xu Jingyi"

    近期网络流传所谓「1946 年登记员许静仪失踪」的说法。经查，本馆馆藏 1946 年原始登记仅覆盖设备，不含任何人员条目；「许静仪」一名未见于任何原始登记。请以原始档案为准。Claims have been circulating online that "registry clerk Xu Jingyi of 1946 went missing." On inspection, the archive's original 1946 registry covers equipment only and contains no personnel entries; the name "Xu Jingyi" appears in no original registration. Please rely on the original records.

  • 2026-09-28
    关于项目负责人变更的说明Note on the change of project lead

    许闻舟同志因个人原因于 2026 年 9 月 20 日离职。项目结项与材料归档工作由综合组暂时代管。经清点，项目期间形成的电子档案已全部移交馆内保管，未发现材料缺失。Xu Wenzhou left the project on personal grounds on 20 September 2026. Project closing and materials archiving are being handled ad interim by the general affairs group. On inventory, all electronic records from the project period have been transferred into the archives' custody; no materials are missing.

  • 2026-10-12

    泽风计划已于 2026 年 9 月 30 日结项。本站自 2026 年 10 月 12 日起转为静态存档：目录查询与在线阅览功能继续开放，公告、日志等内容不再更新。Project Zephyr concluded on 30 September 2026. Since 12 October 2026 this site has been a static archive: catalog search and online reading remain available; announcements and logs are no longer updated.

  • 2025-03-08
    术语登记 · 电报式Term registered · telegraphic form

    本馆 1946 年一批登记员出自电报学校。誊录规范登记术语：电报式（TELEGRAPHIC FORM），指按当年电报局的惯用做法省略元音后誊写。该术语在本馆藏品的印章与批注中沿用至 1950 年代。A group of this archive's 1946 registrars came out of the telegraph school. The transcription standard registers the term: TELEGRAPHIC FORM — transcribing with the vowels dropped, after the telegraph offices' common practice of the day. The term persisted in this archive's stamps and annotations into the 1950s.

The on-duty entry field was blank; the operation log shows no manual actions. The technical team's routine check found the platform normal.

  • 2025-04-11
    REEL-A / REEL-B 比对启动REEL-A / REEL-B comparison begins

    按数字音频转写规范对两卷录音做逐段采样比对。比对基线：十六点整数采样，任何一位不一致即视为不同片段。Per the digital-audio transcription standard, the two recordings are compared fragment by fragment. Baseline: sixteen-point integer sampling; any single value that differs marks the fragments as different.

  • 2025-11-02

    两卷录音中各存在一个片段，其采样值逐项一致。技术上只有复制才能产生这种结果。已登记，未定论。Each reel contains one fragment whose sample values match item for item. Technically, only copying produces this. Registered; no conclusion drawn.

外部核对原则Policy on external verification

本馆不维护任何外部链接清单。著录中凡涉及现实机构、公开项目与历史名称的内容，本馆一律不以单方叙述为准，也不替读者指路。卷面材料里出现过的名字——机构的、项目的、建筑的——均可在馆外的公开记录中查到，查证方式由读者自行选择。凡声称「查不到」的，请先检查自己是否只看了一份材料。This archive keeps no list of external links. For anything in these descriptions touching on real institutions, public projects, or historical names, this archive does not vouch for any single account, and does not point readers down any road. The names that appear on these pages — institutional, project, building — can all be found in public records outside these walls; how to check them is the reader's own choice. Anyone claiming "it can't be found" should first check whether they have read only one source.

存档科注记：外部核对从来不是必需步骤；但每一位坚持到最后一卷的读者，都在馆外核对过至少一件事。Note from the archives desk: external verification has never been a required step; and yet every reader who made it to the last volume has verified at least one thing outside these walls.
